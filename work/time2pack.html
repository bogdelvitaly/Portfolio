Product Design · UX/UI Design

Time2pack

An all-in-one travel companion that helps people plan trips, build packing lists, and keep every travel detail in one place.

Product StrategyUX ResearchPrototypingMobile

Challenge

Travelers juggle multiple apps, notes and spreadsheets to prepare for a trip. Packing lists go stale, documents scatter across services, and flight information is hard to track in one place. The challenge was combining these workflows into one lightweight, accessible, enjoyable product.

Solution

The app brings together the core travel scenarios in a single flow:

  • Smart, customizable packing lists
  • Packing progress tracking
  • Flight tracking with real-time alerts
  • Weather forecasts for travel dates
  • Travel documents and ticket storage
  • Notes, photos, and offline access to trip information

Design Approach

Information is grouped around the trip itself, keeping navigation intuitive and cognitive load low. Particular attention went to:

  • Fast onboarding with no mandatory registration
  • Clear information hierarchy
  • Editable, reusable packing templates
  • Visual progress tracking while packing
  • Offline access for reliability while traveling
  • No ads, no unnecessary complexity

Result

Time2Pack grew into a travel companion used by tens of thousands of people worldwide, with regular updates that keep improving how they plan and pack.
