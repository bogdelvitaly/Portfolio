Insurance · UX/UI Design — 01 / 06

Risk Calculation

A web application that turns insurance risk assessment into a handful of inputs — built for the managers who help people get insured, not for actuaries.

UI/UX DesignVisual DirectionInteraction Design

Wireframes

We collected the requirements and started developing prototypes on top of them — first the controls, general navigation, and status displays, then a first draft of the risk-identification map. Visual interactions and a UI kit followed.

Prototype

The prototype started as a structural outline. Once the client approved it, I moved to detailed prototypes built from realistic components — some elements were simplified, others rethought completely.

Visual Design

Risk needed to be readable at a glance, so color carries meaning throughout: you can tell which risk group something belongs to without reading a label. A UI kit followed — colors, type, components and their states, documented for reuse.

Result

Managers can now process a client's risk profile in under 2 minutes — down from around 10. That difference shows up directly in how many contracts they can close in a day.
