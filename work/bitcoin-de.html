Overview

Making a volatile market feel steady and legible

Bitcoin.de needed a convenient, trustworthy Android app for cryptocurrency trading: one that supported the essential financial workflows and stayed approachable for users at any level of experience.

01 – Key features

Essential workflows, clear at every step

The app covers the work people do with their crypto every day.

  1. 01

    Buying and selling

    Cryptocurrencies.

  2. 02

    Portfolio and balance

    Management.

  3. 03

    Price alerts

    Market monitoring.

  4. 04

    Transactions and notifications

    Clear flows.

02 – Design approach

Simplicity, trust and efficiency

The experience was built around three principles: simplicity, trust and efficiency.

  • Clear information hierarchy for financial data
  • Intuitive trading and transaction flows
  • Android-native navigation patterns
  • Readable portfolio and balance views
  • Transparent states and confirmations for critical actions

Result

The updated Android app expanded Bitcoin.de's ecosystem and attracted more than 10,000 users – giving them a clearer, more convenient way to manage their crypto.
