Currency · UX/UI Design

Bitcoin DE

An Android cryptocurrency trading experience built to make a volatile market feel steady and legible.

Interaction DesignVisual DesignAndroid

Challenge

Bitcoin.de needed a convenient, trustworthy Android app for cryptocurrency trading — one that supported essential financial workflows while staying approachable for users at any experience level. Key features:

  • Buying and selling cryptocurrencies
  • Portfolio and balance management
  • Price alerts and market monitoring
  • Clear transaction and notification flows

Design Approach

The experience was built around three principles: simplicity, trust, and efficiency.

  • Clear information hierarchy for financial data
  • Intuitive trading and transaction flows
  • Android-native navigation patterns
  • Readable portfolio and balance views
  • Transparent states and confirmations for critical actions

Result

The updated Android app expanded Bitcoin.de's ecosystem and attracted more than 10,000 users — giving them a clearer, more convenient way to manage their crypto.
