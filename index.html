About

Crafting systems that hold up under real use.

I'm Vitaly, a UX/UI and product designer who cares more about whether something works than whether it looks clever. Most of my work sits in domains people don't love thinking about — insurance, logistics, currency, HR — which is exactly why the interface has to do the thinking for them.

I blend strategy, research and craft to take a product from a messy problem to something people trust on the first try.

  • 01

    Visual Systems

    I build interfaces that hold together: reusable components, clear hierarchy, and typography that makes a product feel considered before anyone reads a word.

  • 02

    Product Thinking

    I turn ambiguous problems into shippable products — balancing user needs, business goals and engineering constraints into one direction.

  • 03

    User Experience

    Good UX disappears. I use research and prototyping to make interactions feel obvious, whether it's a trading app or an internal ops tool.

  • 04

    Research & Iteration

    I test assumptions against real behavior, not opinions, and keep refining after launch — not just before it.
